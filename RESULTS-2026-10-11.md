## Reading the numbers

- **Rabi** validates the full chain: the −1.53% matches the predicted
zero-padded-FFT estimator quantization (−1.54%) to the digit on real hardware.
- **Ramsey** is a consistency check, not a precision measurement: the chain
extracted a frequency in the right neighborhood of the programmed 250 kHz.
The −9.8% deviation exceeds estimator error and is logged as a real effect
(dephasing on a below-average qubit; see T1).
- **T1 = 78.7 µs** is the headline measurement: a below-average Heron qubit
(typical medians 150–250 µs), consistent with the Ramsey deviation.
- **Coherent-ops budget (measured):** 78.7 µs / 60 ns ≈ **1.3×10³** gate
operations per T1; T2-bounded at ≤2.6×10³. Against BASE's antiproton
(~5×10⁴): same qualitative regime — many coherent ops per coherence time —
at a smaller budget. The ratio, not the raw seconds, is the analogy's payload.
